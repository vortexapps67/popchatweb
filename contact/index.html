what happens next.

we read everything that comes in. we are a small team, so replies can be slower than a support desk with a queue — if something is urgent, say so in the subject line and we will treat it as urgent.

to help you fastest, it helps to include:

  • your handle, if you have one.
  • which device and version of the app you are on.
  • what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • a screenshot, if there is one.

if you found a security problem

please email us directly and say so in the first line. we will treat it as a priority and we will not ask you to prove anything before we look at it.

if you would rather read the rules first, the privacy policy and terms of service are both written in plain language on purpose.
