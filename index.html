01 · identity

a number is not an identity.

most messengers treat your phone number as the account. Omi Chat treats it as optional — because for a lot of people, handing over a number was the exact part they wanted to skip.

what we ask for

  • handlerequired
  • phone numbernot required
  • carrier / simnot required

what we don’t build

no “people you may know” scraped from your address book, and no number-based discovery. your contacts stay on your phone, where they already were.

what leaving looks like

no number tied to the account means no number attached to a delete request. remove the handle, take the messages with you.

02 · the thread
